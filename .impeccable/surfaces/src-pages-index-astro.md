Scope: the whole clinic demo site, one page (`src/pages/index.astro`). Mode: Persuade.

Audience: clinic owners judging a demo on a phone; story visitors are Lahore patients on budget Android phones.
Job: show that the clinic site books a visit by itself. Action: pick a doctor, day and free time, then confirm.
Proof: a booking flow that works end to end, real looking fees, timings, doctors and a drawn route.
Constraints: everything labelled demo, no real numbers, simulated WhatsApp/call/directions, no medical claims.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The clinic as cool glazed kashi tilework: every free appointment is a glazed tile you can take. It refuses the stock dentist-smile hero with a Book button and icon service cards.

OWN-WORLD: White glaze ground (#f5f7f2), lapis ink (#1d3a8a family) for text, turquoise glaze for free tiles, ochre for filling up, matte terracotta brick for taken. Thin grout lines join every caption to its tile. A Mughal jali lattice frames the booking panel. Solid lapis appears once: the confirmed booking token.

STORY: A patient sees today's free times at once, learns fees and doctors without calling, books in a few taps, and knows how to reach the door. A clinic owner sees the mechanism working.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left, the promise headline and timings and fee line. Right (below on phones), a jali-framed board: day tiles with a fill ladder, doctor switch, and today's slot tiles. The first free tile carries Book.

FORM: Kashi glaze and jali screen, index 4 on the ranked list, seed key e6f21656. Signature interaction: picking a slot glazes the tile with a light sweep.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
