# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Delegated: Astro (static pages, fast on cheap phones, good for Google), small client scripts only where the demo needs them, hosted on Cloudflare Pages. The user left the stack to us; it matches the other demo sites.

## Users

- **Real audience: clinic owners and doctors in Pakistan** whom the software house shows this demo to, usually on a phone during a meeting or over WhatsApp. They judge in a minute whether they want "this, for my clinic".
- **Story audience: patients and families in Lahore** looking for a dentist, mostly on budget Android phones. They want to know the timings, fees, location and doctors, and to book a visit without a phone call.

## Product Purpose

A demonstration website for a fictional dental and family clinic, built by the software house to show what a clinic website from them looks like and does. Success: a clinic owner asks for one like it.

## Positioning

A complete clinic website that books appointments by itself (pick a doctor, a day and a free time), with WhatsApp and directions one tap away, rather than a brochure with a phone number.

## Operating Context

Shown live from a link. Visitors arrive from Google Maps, Facebook and WhatsApp shares. Pakistani conventions: +92 numbers, PKR fees, Pakistan time, Monday to Saturday clinic days, evening slots after office hours.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Every page is a demo: the clinic, doctors, prices, reviews and numbers are made up and must be labelled as a demo where a visitor could mistake them for real.
- No real phone or WhatsApp number anywhere. Booking, WhatsApp and call actions show what would happen on a real site instead of sending anything.
- No medical claims, cures, guarantees or "best in Lahore" style claims.
- English, with room for an Urdu version on a real build.

## Evidence on Hand

None. No real clinic, doctors, reviews or photos. Photos come from free stock libraries (Pexels or Unsplash licences); everything else is authored demo content.

## Product Principles

1. Trust at first sight: calm, clean and specific (real looking timings, fees and doctor details) beats decoration.
2. Phone first: a patient books in a few taps with one thumb.
3. Honest demo: never let a visitor believe the clinic is real.
4. Show the mechanism: the booking flow is the selling point, so it works end to end.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Large tap targets, readable type for older patients, works on slow connections and small screens.
